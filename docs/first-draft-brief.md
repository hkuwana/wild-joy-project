# Wild Joy first draft

Outcome: a visitor can understand Alyssa’s retreats, evaluate Bali without hunting through repeated sections, and contact her with a room preference.

Three useful rungs: (1) organized static site with source and free hosting, (2) a visual content editor and repeatable retreat publishing, (3) an end-to-end booking and trip operations system. This iteration chooses rung 1. Booking/payment systems introduce operational work that a first visual draft should not pretend to solve.

Finish line: responsive four-page site, source-grounded itinerary/prices/fit guidance, working inquiry and calendar/print controls, public MIT implementation repository and a separate Cloudflare draft URL. Keep existing domain intact. Stop after browser review and deployment verification.

Design candidates: a travel journal; a coastal guesthouse welcome book; independent travel magazine; analog photo album; photographic travel correspondence; a gallery exhibition; a joyful regional festival program. The fifth direction is travel correspondence: oversized expressive serif headlines, deep rust ink on warm ivory, photo-led compositions, restrained coral and pool-green sections, dated invitations and generous editorial spacing. This is a website with conventional navigation, not a skeuomorphic postcard. Source photos carry the visual identity.

FORM: seed:ca175f114bbd1f61. Code-first selected by Hiro. Proceeding on his delegated recommendation rather than requiring another design approval.

Challenge assessment: magazine is competitive for clarity, but more impersonal; keep its strong hierarchy. Risograph is competitive for warmth but would distort photos; keep its commitment to a limited palette. Hybrid collage, phosphor terminal, alien forest and Akari sculpture lose product clarity and audience identification; retain disciplined focus, visible state, navigable paths and readable unfolding content, without their visual costumes.

Expert move: place trip suitability and exclusions alongside prices; preserve all eight itinerary days and publish no speculative booking status. Belief to test: existing content is enough to make the decision clear; check rendered navigation and complete source coverage. External evidence: browser at desktop/mobile widths; source ledger; local build/link checks; live deployment response.

Limit: no payments, registrations, CMS accounts, newsletter collection, invented reviews, new legal policies, domain transfer, or production replacement. Photos and text are not open licensed by the code license.
